How it works

Pick the player you think will win a game that has just started. Every bet is the same: 20 DOGE, paid from your wallet. Betting stays open for the first part of the game (one sixth of its length), then it closes and everyone waits for the result.

20% of all bets stays with the game (prize pool of the next tournaments).
20% goes to the player who won the game as a bonus — at most half of that tournament's first prize.
The rest is shared by the people who picked the winner, and they never get less than their own bet back.
Refund if fewer than 3 people bet, if nobody picked the winner, or if everybody picked the same player.

Players of a game cannot bet on it. One bet per wallet per game. Winnings are collected with the gold Claim button in the top right corner.
