🏅 Mini-Tournament Rules

Tickets, game format and DOGE prize payouts in Mini-Tournaments

🎫 Registration and tickets

  • Entering the matchmaking queue for a Mini-Tournament room requires a Mini-Tournament Ticket, bought in the Shop tab with your DOGE balance. The current ticket price is shown live in the Shop and on the Mini-Tournaments page before you buy.
  • One Mini-Tournament Ticket entitles you to enter the queue for one room of your choice. A player can hold at most 1 unused ticket at a time — another one can be bought only after the previous one has been used or after leaving the queue before the round starts. There is no deadline for using a ticket you hold.
  • The ticket is actually used only when the round starts (once all 10 players have been matched and have accepted the match — see the “Matchmaking” section below). Until then you can safely click “Cancel search” without losing the ticket.
  • The number of tickets that can be bought in a given calendar month is limited by a total monthly pool set by the administrators — its current state is visible live in the Service before you buy. Not every Mini-Tournament room requires a ticket — some rooms may be free, depending on the room settings.
The full terms of purchase, payment, withdrawal from the contract and complaints for Mini-Tournament Tickets are set out in the Terms of Service (§5a–§6).

🎯 Matchmaking

  • After clicking “Find a Game” the player enters the room’s matchmaking queue — they do not join a visible participant list straight away. While waiting and while confirming the match you do not see the nicknames, avatars or statistics of other waiting or matched players — the full line-up of the round is revealed only once it has actually started.
  • The system matches a group of 10 players based on a separate, dedicated Mini-Tournament MMR ranking (independent of the Overall Ranking — it starts at 1000 points and changes only with Mini-Tournament round results). The matching range grows with waiting time: up to 30 seconds ±50 MMR points, from 31 to 60 seconds ±150 points, and after 60 seconds the system matches any available player from the queue so that nobody waits forever.
  • When the system has a group of 10 players, each of them gets 15 seconds to confirm (“Accept”). If everyone accepts, the Mini-Tournament Ticket is used, the players enter the room and the line-up is revealed.
  • If even one player does not accept in time, they are removed from the queue and can start searching again, while the other players who accepted automatically return to the queue with priority in the next match — without losing their ticket and without having to click “Find a Game” again.

🗓️ Game format

Each Mini-Tournament room is a single round played under the same rules as the regular Battle Rooms (building your village, troops, combat, conquest with the Lord) — the full description of the mechanics is in the Game rules and mechanics. The room’s final ranking is determined by the same ranking rules as in a regular game (including village points).

The round length and the number of players in a room are set by the administrators when creating the Mini-Tournament — they are visible in the Service before you join. The game pace (building, recruiting, economy, troop travel time) is sped up or slowed down in proportion to the round length so that the game stays consistent regardless of its length — the current factor is shown on the room tile as “Speed”.

🏆 Prizes

Every Mini-Tournament room has a pre-set, guaranteed DOGE prize for 1st, 2nd and 3rd place, announced by the administrators before registration for that room opens — you will see the exact breakdown on the room tile in the Mini-Tournaments tab before you join. The prize amounts do not depend on the number of tickets sold or the number of participants. Prizes are paid automatically to the player’s DOGE balance right after the room’s round ends, in the full gross amount (100%) — the Service does not withhold any tax.

How 1st, 2nd and 3rd place are decided: above all by the village points earned in the round — whoever has the most takes the higher place. If two or more players earn exactly the same village points, their order is decided in turn by: 1) the total number of enemy units defeated (attack + defence), 2) the number of units defeated in attack, 3) the number of units defeated in defence, 4) who reached that number of points earlier in the round. Each of these criteria is your real result in the game — none of them is random or depends on who signed up or registered first. This way the result can always be fully explained with concrete numbers, and an “unbreakable” tie practically cannot happen.

The Organiser is a private individual not running a business and does not act as an income tax remitter — the winner settles the winnings in their own annual tax return (in Poland: PIT, art. 20(1) of the PIT Act). You can withdraw your accumulated DOGE balance to your DOGE address in the Wallet at any time — details in the Terms of Service (§5b).

🤝 Referral Programme

  • Every player has a unique referral code in the Profile tab, which they can share with friends before they register in the Service.
  • When 10 people referred with a given code buy any ticket (Tournament or Mini-Tournament — either one), the referring player automatically receives a DOGE bonus on their balance — the bonus repeats for every further 10 referred people, with no upper limit.
  • The bonus depends only on the verified number of purchases made by referred people and is not awarded by lottery or any element of chance.

🚫 Fair play

The same fair play rules apply in Mini-Tournaments as in the regular game — including the ban on using multiple accounts (multi-accounting).

  • The system automatically and continuously detects attempts to play from two or more accounts from the same IP address (and from the same device) within the same round.
  • Detecting such an attempt results in an immediate block of all linked accounts and the loss of the Mini-Tournament Ticket held, with no right to a refund.
  • Independently of this, the general rules and consequences for multi-accounting described in the Terms of Service and in the “Fair play and consequences of cheating” section of the Game rules and mechanics also apply (including a possible permanent ban across the whole service and the loss of accumulated Skill Points).
Trying to get around this protection by logging into a second account from a different IP address (e.g. from another network/another device outside your home) is equally forbidden and treated as a breach of fair play — if it is established by other means, the consequences are the same as with automatic detection.
